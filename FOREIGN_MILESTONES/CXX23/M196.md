# CXX23/M196 — `requires` clauses

**Introduced:** C++20. Present in C++23 (ISO/IEC 14882:2024).
**Answer: satellite milestone [M31](../SATELLITE/M31-user-generics.md).**

## C++

```cpp
template <typename T> requires std::integral<T> T half(T);
```

## Why

Constraints come after generics: [M36](M36.md) (concepts) is [M31](../SATELLITE/M31-user-generics.md), after generics.
