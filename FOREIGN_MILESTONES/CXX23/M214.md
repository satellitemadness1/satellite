# CXX23/M214 — `std::exception_ptr`, `rethrow`, nested exceptions

**Introduced:** C++11. Present in C++23 (ISO/IEC 14882:2024).
**Answer: satellite milestone [M33](../SATELLITE/M33-catching-a-refusal.md).**

## C++

```cpp
std::exception_ptr e = std::current_exception();
```

## Why

Carrying an error to another thread or another place is what [M33](../SATELLITE/M33-catching-a-refusal.md)'s value-shaped refusal would allow. Before [M33](../SATELLITE/M33-catching-a-refusal.md) there is nothing to carry.
