# CXX23/M116 — designated initialisers `.x = 1`

**Introduced:** C++20. Present in C++23 (ISO/IEC 14882:2024).
**Answer: open — no milestone and no refusal yet; this needs a decision.**

## C++

```cpp
Point p{.x = 1, .y = 2};
```

## Why

Follows aggregate initialisation above. Naming each field at the construction site is the readable form of that feature, and it fits satellite's explicit style.
