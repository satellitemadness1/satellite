# CXX23/M285 — `std::complex`

**Introduced:** C++98. Present in C++23 (ISO/IEC 14882:2024).
**Answer: never.**

## C++

```cpp
std::complex<double> z{1, 2};
```

## Why

A library, not a language feature, which is the CXX26 folder's answer for `std::linalg`. A spacesuit with two numbers is the satellite version, and [M38](../SATELLITE/M38-operator-overloading.md) would give it `+`.
