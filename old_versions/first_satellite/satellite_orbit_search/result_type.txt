satellite orbit — the result type, its weight, and its attention
================================================================
Written 2026-08-24, straight after the engine landed, as the spec for the next
piece of work. BUILT 2026-08-25 -- the half below the line is the record, and
`make test` is green with all nineteen binaries, orbit_test included.

Read orbit.txt first (the intent), then orbit_plan.txt (what the five phases
are and what landed). This file is only about what a RESULT is.


WHAT WAS ASKED FOR
------------------
Verbatim, because the two new words are the whole point and paraphrasing them
would lose it:

    "build the result type; so it has to be iterable; it has to be ranked,
     it's results have to have weight or something, and they have to have
     another property attention, so we are kinda making an ai, and the
     attention needs to be like, based on concrete things, and the weight
     based on what the phases think that that result is; so each real
     'result' will have a weight and it will have an attention -- the
     attention is specifically the concrete stuff, and the weight is what
     orbit 'thinks' of the results"

And the purpose, from the same message: "We are doing this for the purpose of
trying to get a type of AI working."


THE SPLIT, AND WHY IT IS THE GOOD IDEA IN THIS DESIGN
-----------------------------------------------------
    DECISION 1. ATTENTION IS COUNTED. WEIGHT IS JUDGED.

    attention   what is literally, checkably true about this result in the
                data in front of us. Countable. If every phase's opinion were
                deleted, attention would still be computable from the corpus
                alone.

    weight      what orbit THINKS this result is. The phases' judgement:
                tightness, how many of them agreed, what memory says, and
                whether the engine proposed it rather than found it.

The reason to carry BOTH rather than one blended number is that THE TWO
DISAGREEING IS THE USEFUL SIGNAL, and it is the signal an AI most needs to be
able to emit about itself:

    high attention, low weight    it is definitely in there, a lot, and orbit
                                  does not think it is what you meant
    high weight, low attention    orbit thinks this is the answer and there is
                                  almost nothing concrete under it

The second line is the dangerous one, and today's single `confidence` number
CANNOT EXPRESS IT. A constructed neighbour that three phases like and that
occurs twice, and a direct hit that occurs twice, can land on the same
confidence by different routes, and nothing in the answer says which happened.
Splitting the number is what makes "orbit is speculating" a thing a program can
test for rather than a thing a person has to infer from `why`.

That is also the honest reading of "we are kinda making an ai": the interesting
property of one is not that it answers, it is that it can say how much of the
answer is grounded.

    DECISION 2. ATTENTION MAY NEVER READ A PHASE'S OPINION.

Not a style rule. If attention were allowed to include agreement or memory, the
two numbers would share inputs and could no longer disagree in the way DECISION
1 needs them to -- and the pair would be two spellings of one judgement rather
than a judgement and its evidence. Attention is computed from the corpus and
the walk. Nothing else.


WHAT THE FIELDS BECOME
----------------------
The engine already computes eleven fields per finding (orbit.hpp, struct
Finding). They sort cleanly, which is the sign the split is real rather than
imposed:

    ATTENTION, the concrete    occurrence  how many times it literally occurs
                               path        where it literally is
                               key         what it is literally stored under
                               constructed whether it is in the corpus AT ALL

    WEIGHT, the judged         score       the ladder rung it matched at
                               agreement   how many phases concluded it
                               prior       what memory says it has meant
                               phase       which phase's judgement this is
                               why         the sentence

`confidence` as it exists today is a blend of all of both and becomes WEIGHT.
`occurrence` stops feeding weight and becomes the spine of attention -- which
is a real behaviour change and is the point, not a side effect.

    DECISION 2a. ATTENTION IS A RAW COUNT. WEIGHT IS 0..100. DECIDED.

Asked and answered: attention is a count. "It is in there 4 times" is a FACT,
and "attention 62" is a rescaling of that fact which has thrown away the number
it came from. A count also cannot be quietly reweighted later without somebody
noticing, which a percentage can.

They are DELIBERATELY not the same kind of number, and that is a feature rather
than an inconsistency to tidy up: it means nobody can add them together, average
them, or sort on their sum. DECISION 1 says the pair disagreeing is the product,
and two numbers on the same scale are an invitation to blend them.

The cost, stated: attention does not compare across corpora. Four occurrences in
a list of seven and four in a list of seven thousand are the same attention and
are not the same fact. A program that needs the ratio has .length() and can
divide; the engine will not do it silently.


ITERABLE, AND RANKED
--------------------
    DECISION 3. BOTH, AND THE QUESTION DISSOLVES. DECIDED.

Asked as "fields or both or alternatives, I don't know" -- and the answer is
that it is not a choice, because the two are reached by different spellings and
one of them is already free.

**Verified** in the tree: the language has NO foreach. Iteration is an indexed
`satellite.statement.for` over anything answering .length() and [i]. And a map
already answers .keys(), .values() and .length() (src/evaluator/maps.cpp).

So, because a result IS a map underneath:

    FIELDS        already iterable. r.keys(), r.values(), r.length(), r["why"].
                  Inherited from the map substrate. NOTHING TO BUILD.
    ALTERNATIVES  r.alternatives(), answering a ranked list of results, which
                  the same indexed loop walks.

Spending the type's own spelling on fields would buy nothing that the map
substrate does not already give away. Spending it on alternatives buys the one
thing that cannot otherwise be reached. That is the whole argument.

    DECISION 3a. r.length() COUNTS FIELDS, NOT ALTERNATIVES.

It is the map's answer and a result is a map, so consistency decides it. The
surprise is real and is worth naming: `found.length()` is how many results came
back, and `r.length()` is how many fields one result has. The alternative
reading -- length means alternatives -- would make a result a map that lies
about its own size, and every other map method would still be counting fields.
A program that wants the other number writes r.alternatives().length(), which
says which number it wants.

    DECISION 3b. r[0] STAYS A KEY LOOKUP. IT IS NOT ALTERNATIVE ZERO.

The search power's DECISION 6a spends its whole argument refusing to let a
whole-number subscript change meaning with context, and this is the same rule:
a result is a map, a map's subscript is by key, and `r[0]` is the key 0. Making
it mean "the first alternative" would be exactly the action at a distance that
decision exists to prevent.


WHY THIS IS A TYPE AND NOT MORE MAP KEYS
----------------------------------------
Measured, not argued. **Verified** against ./satl on 2026-08-24: a result
cannot be BOUND TO A TYPED VARIABLE AT ALL today.

    satellite.container.list<satellite.container.map<
        satellite.variable.string, satellite.variable.string>> found
        = flags.orbit(want)

    satellite: cannot initialise ... with [{value: [true, true], key: nil, ...}]

The values are heterogeneous -- a list, a nil, a number, a bool, a string -- and
the type system has no way to say so. Results are reachable ONLY by chaining:
`flags.orbit(want)[0]["why"]`. Note that `.search()`'s rich form has had exactly
this limitation since it landed; search_test never binds one either. A named
type fixes both at once, and it is the reason the type is worth its cost.

The second reason: `.why()` and `.weight()` as METHODS cannot collide with a
user's own map keys, and as keys they can.


THE MECHANICS, ALREADY CHECKED
------------------------------
All **verified** in the tree on 2026-08-24, so the next session does not have to
re-derive them:

 1. src/satellite_value/value_variant.hpp is marked APPEND ONLY and says why:
    help_for() and module_of() switch on RAW variant indices, so appending is
    free and inserting renumbers everything after it. ResultRef is index 11.

 2. EXACTLY TWO places in the whole tree read a raw variant index --
    module_of() in src/evaluator/types.cpp and help_for() in
    src/evaluator/help.cpp. Both get a `case 11:`.

 3. A shared_ptr is 16 bytes and the variant already holds five, so
    sizeof(Value) STAYS 40 and ast_test's static_assert keeps holding. An
    alternative that grew it would be refused on that ground alone -- §8.1's
    Number migration and §10's Expr/Value split both rest on 40.

 4. THE PRECEDENT IS `arguments`, and it should be followed exactly. ArgsRef is
    index 10: a list<string> to matches(), `satellite.container.arguments` to
    anyone reading an error, with a method table a list does not have. A result
    is the same shape one level over: a MAP to matches(), so it type-checks,
    prints and is searchable as one; `satellite.container.result` to a reader;
    with .weight(), .attention(), .why(), .alternatives() that a map lacks.

 5. THIS BREAKS THE ONE-FOLDER RULE, and deliberately. orbit was asked to be new
    work in satellite_orbit_search/ and the engine is. A variant alternative
    cannot be: it must edit src/satellite_value/, src/evaluator/types.cpp and
    src/evaluator/help.cpp. Those three edits are the price of the type and
    there is no version of it that avoids them. Confirm before spending them.


WHAT NOT TO DO
--------------
**Do not blend weight and attention into one number for ranking.** Phase 5 has
to rank on something, and the temptation will be to add them. DECISION 1 exists
because the pair disagreeing is the product; a ranking that averages them
destroys exactly the case the split was built to expose. Rank on weight, and let
attention be read beside it.

**Do not let attention count a constructed result as present.** A constructed
neighbour occurs as a WINDOW the perturbed query matched, which is a real count
about a value that is not in the corpus as a unit. It is already stored in
`occurrence` by phase 3 and must stay distinguishable from a value that is
literally there -- `constructed` is that flag and it belongs to attention.

**Do not renumber the variant.** See mechanic 1. It is silent and it breaks
help and error messages rather than the build.

    DECISION 4a. THE FIELDS MAP IS THE ONLY STORAGE. DECIDED WHILE BUILDING.

A ResultBody holds a map Value and not sixteen typed members. `r.why()` and
`r["why"]` are one lookup in one place, so they cannot come to disagree, and a
field added to the builder is a method the same day -- the method table IS the
fields map, walked by name. Sixteen typed members beside a map that repeated
them would have been two sources of truth for one answer.

    DECISION 4b. SIXTEEN FIELDS, AND TWO OF THEM ARE THE QUESTION.

    value key path | attention constructed | weight score phase agreement
    prior why phases | pattern threshold | rank candidates

`pattern` and `threshold` are new here and are not in the spec above. A result
handed to a capsule ON ITS OWN is otherwise an answer with no question, and
"carries the whole state of the search" cannot mean anything less than knowing
what was asked and at what dial.

`phases` is the other addition, and it is the request's "it must encompass the
5 phases" taken literally: a map of all five names to what each concluded, nil
where that phase never reached this answer. ALL FIVE ARE PRESENT, because §8.6
makes a missing key an error and "predict had nothing to say about this" is an
answer a program has to be able to ask for without risking one.

    DECISION 4c. ATTENTION COUNTS WINDOWS, AND THAT FIXED A REAL BACKWARDS.

    FOUND BY BUILDING IT, not by reading the spec.

occurrence was looked up in the corpus ALPHABET, which holds nodes. A run is a
window over a list and never a node, so [true, true] -- the thing orbit.txt's
own example asks for -- scored occurrence 0 while the constructed [true, false]
scored 2, because phase 3 counted its own windows on the way past. The invented
answer looked more present than the found one, in the number whose whole job is
to say what is really there.

orbit_sightings counts both kinds, at level 1, for every finding alike, and
phase 3 no longer writes a count at all. Both now read attention 2, which is
the truth about that corpus -- and orbit_test pins it.

    DECISION 4d. THE WEIGHTS RENORMALISE TO 100 RATHER THAN SUMMING TO 85.

Occurrence's 15 left with it. Tightness stays at 50 -- half of everything,
because the ladder is the one signal that says the two values are alike -- and
the 15 went to agreement (step 10 -> 15, cap unchanged at two extra phases,
which is where its own argument put it) and to prior (15 -> 20). A maximum
nobody can reach is not a scale.

    DECISION 4e. ALTERNATIVES ARE ONE LEVEL DEEP, BY CONSTRUCTION.

Every result carries every other, so a result alone can still say what else the
search considered -- which the spec calls the one thing that cannot otherwise be
reached. If the ones it carried also carried it, the handles would point in a
ring and the resolution would leak: the same shared_ptr cycle value.hpp calls
the honest cost of an Object, arrived at from a different direction.

So there are two results per finding sharing one fields map: a BARE one, which
is what an alternative is, and the one in the returned list, which holds a
shared handle to the ranked list of bare ones plus its own rank. Storage is
linear in the finding count rather than square in it, `.alternatives()` is that
list with `rank` left out, and an alternative's own .alternatives() is empty.

`candidates` is a count of what the RESOLUTION produced and not of what this
result can hand back, which is what lets the twins share one fields map and
makes them compare equal -- they are the same answer to the same question.

    DECISION 8. `make test` WAS RUNNING A STALE BINARY.

    FOUND BY RUNNING IT. Recorded because the symptom was a GREEN line.

TESTBINS in 120-tests.mk was spelled out by hand and had never gained
search_test or orbit_test, both of which the `test` recipe RUNS. So those two
were run and never built: this session renamed Finding::confidence, orbit_test
should have failed to COMPILE, and the suite went on printing PASS from a binary
compiled the previous day.

TESTBINS is derived from TESTNAMES now, which is what 070-directories.mk always
claimed to be "the single place a test is declared to exist". The run ORDER
below it stays written out, because it is an order and not a set -- library_test
first and its sanitized twin second -- but it can no longer run something nobody
built.

The file's own comment warned about exactly this: ".gitignore's own note records
what a second hand-copied list costs". It was right, and it was describing
itself.


WHAT THIS DOES NOT DO
---------------------
**`.search()`'s rich form still answers maps.** The spec says a named type fixes
that limitation too, and it is not fixed. A search hit knows a value, a key, a
path and a score; nine of a result's sixteen fields would be nil, and attention
would need the corpus walk that `.search()` exists to avoid. A type most of
whose fields are empty for one of its two producers is a type stretched over two
things. The binding gap for `.search()` is real and is still open.

**Writing through a result.** A result carries a path, which is what a
write-through needs. Still not built, here or in the search power.

**A help topic for the ladder and the phases.** help_for() knows the result type
now, so `satellite.help(found[0])` answers. Nothing yet explains the ten levels
or the five phases as a TOPIC -- search_power.txt's item 9, still owed.
