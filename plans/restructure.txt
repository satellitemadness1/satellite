THE TWO SINGLE-FUNCTION FILES — the pattern that worked
-------------------------------------------------------
call_module was 771 lines in ONE function; call_method was 715. Neither could
be split by moving text, because the branches all `return` out of the middle
of a function. The transformation that made the bodies move BYTE FOR BYTE:

  each arm returns std::optional<ValuePtr>

  std::nullopt          = "not mine, keep looking"
  engaged optional      = this arm answered
  engaged, holding null = it answered by FAILING (§8.3.1: a failure is a
                          value, and fail() has already run)

Because ValuePtr converts implicitly to an engaged optional, every single
`return <expr>;` inside a moved branch needed NO EDIT. That is the whole
trick, and it is the reason to reach for optional here rather than a
bool-plus-out-parameter.

For call_method the same problem appeared one level down: the branches used
two lambdas, `arity` and `number_arg`, from a shared prologue. Rather than
rewrite ~50 call sites, the lambdas were promoted to Evaluator methods and
each arm declares a two-line lambda OF THE SAME NAME that forwards to them.
Call sites unchanged.
