# M14 — the console's other half: the reader thread and the terminal's facts

**Landed 2026-09-04** (`9f403f8`) — **in one commit with M13**, behind it the
same sitting. M3-and-M4's precedent, and M13.md carries the same note.

[PLAN.md](../PLAN.md) §8's M14 entry is the specification: **eight paths,
`1 5 2` through `1 5 9`, every child of `console` except `display`** — which
finished the namespace, §2.2 having no tenth child. This is a **review and not
a plan**: what was decided on the way, what building it found, and what waits
on the author.

**The six open questions were answered by the author in one sitting on landing
day**, after the analysis was put to them question by question — and two of
the six turned out to already be answered by standing text: one-reader by
§10.1's own invariant, not-a-pool-tenant by §4.5.1's 2026-09-02 correction,
which the open bullet had gone stale against. PLAN's entry now carries every
answer where its question stood.

Interrupted and the end of input, the two answers §6 said not to get
backwards — and they cannot be: they arrive as different answers now.

## 1. What the milestone was

- **The reader thread** —
  [src/satellite_console/reader.cpp](../src/satellite_console/reader.cpp):
  §10.1's printer with the polarity reversed. It parks in `poll()` on stdin
  AND a self-pipe, never bare in `read(2)`; whole lines go into a queue under
  a lock; the program asks and is answered at once. **One consumer of stdin at
  any instant.** Not a pool tenant, its own thread, joined as the console
  shutdown's fifth step.
- **`input` in three shapes** — `1 5 2`, `1 5 3`, `1 5 4` — one place that
  prompts (un-newlined, one queued piece), one that drains (the barrier), one
  that reads. Three answers, never one empty string: a line, S1001's end, or
  nothing-then-the-boundary for Ctrl-C.
- **`typed()`** `1 5 5` — a line, or nothing, immediately. The one genuinely
  new mechanism: v1 has no non-blocking input of any kind, and M12's nothing
  is consumed here rather than invented.
- **`width`/`height`** `1 5 6`/`1 5 7` — v1's 14 lines of 1,449: the ioctl
  with its 80 fallback, plus the height v1 never had (fallback 24). Facts,
  asked fresh; the pty-resize clause is the proof they are live.
- **`clear()`/`home()`** `1 5 8`/`1 5 9` — queued escapes, never the fd.
  `clear()` homes (the author, with v1's bytes and terminfo's meaning);
  `home()` alone is QUAD's flicker-free repaint.
- **The place parameter** — words.def's third list, `SAT_PLACE`, one row by
  policy, with a `static_assert` in words_invariants.hpp holding it to one.
  The compiler compiles the place as a slot (`op_place`/`op_place_global`,
  the method ops' shape), refuses a non-name (S1002) and any position that
  could read the result (S1003) — both **before the prompt prints**, and both
  provable in a binary that links no console at all, which
  tests/eval_test/compiling.cpp does.
- **The wake pipe** — `system_facts/interrupt.cpp` gained one atomic fd and
  one `write(2)` in the handler; the reader registers its pipe. Ctrl-C wakes
  a blocked `input` no matter which thread the kernel picked, and `EINTR`
  stopped being load-bearing across threads.

## 2. What was decided on the way

- **`1 5 4`'s arity is ONE.** The place never reaches its handler — the op
  owns the write — so `input(prompt)` and `input(prompt, target)` share their
  entire read path and differ only in what the machine does with the answer.
- **An interrupted read answers nothing and the write is SKIPPED.** A Ctrl-C
  at the prompt must not overwrite the place with nothing — the person
  cancelled INTO that variable. A real empty line is an empty string and
  still writes. op_place's comment carries the contract.
- **Interrupted wins over a line that arrived with it** — §10.2 gives the key
  its meaning at the prompt, *cancel*, and acting on an instruction the
  person revoked would be worse than dropping it. reader.cpp says it where
  the order is chosen.
- **The prompt can be any value**, rendered as `display` renders — a number
  can prompt. Same rendering, same seam.
- **`interrupted` reaches the reader through Policy's function pointer**, the
  same seam the boundary and sleep use — so eval_test can interrupt without a
  signal, and `satl` wires `interrupt_requested` in.

## 3. What building it found

- **The doubled backticks and the whole-call road were M13's finds**, one
  session earlier the same day; M14's `input()` `1 5 2` was the second path
  through the whole-call road and paid nothing.
- **A quiet-based pty read never returns against a fast program.** The
  harness's first `gather()` drained "until 50 ms of silence" — against a
  30 ms display cadence that is never. The counter clauses now measure their
  pauses with a deadline (`collect_for`), and pty.hpp records the lesson.
- **`satl` joined a test's dependency list for the first time.**
  console_test's terminal section forkpty(3)s the real binary, so its build
  rule depends on `satl` — an edit to the interpreter re-runs the suite that
  watches it type.

## 4. How it was checked

[tests/console_test/terminal.cpp](../tests/console_test/terminal.cpp) — the
real `satl` under `forkpty(3)`, one fixture per clause, asserted on the
screen. All ten suites pass; the two compile-time refusals run in eval_test,
which links no console — "before the prompt prints" as a link-time fact.

Done-when, clause by clause:

1. **The counter kept rising while `/q` sat half-typed** — a byte, a pause, a
   byte, a pause, and the count of all-digit screen lines grew across every
   pause. QUAD.md §3.4 requirement (1), the claim the milestone exists to
   make. ✓
2. **And nothing spun** — ~60 ms of child CPU across ~1.5 s of waiting,
   measured by `wait4`'s rusage in the same fixture; the suite holds the line
   at 700 ms. This clause is why M13 landed first: the fixture paces with
   `satellite.time.sleep`. ✓
3. **`typed()` told an empty line from no line** — `holding()` answered
   "nothing" before any key, "string" of `size()` 0 after a bare return. ✓
4. **The prompt appeared before the cursor waited** — `who? ` on the screen
   with nothing typed and nothing else to flush it; the drain barrier,
   observable only on a terminal. ✓
5. **`1 5 4` writes a place and yields nothing** — `stars` went in, the
   program displayed it back out of the variable; `input(">", 3)` refused
   S1002 and the assignment refused S1003, both with no prompt printed and
   both in a binary with no console linked. ✓
6. **Ctrl-C was not the end of input** — `0x03` through the pty at the prompt
   answered S0730 and exit 130; `< /dev/null` answered S1001 and exited
   instead of looping. ✓
7. **`width` and `height` were live** — the pty resized 100×40 → 66×22
   between two asks, and both numbers moved with no restart anywhere. ✓
8. **`clear()` and `home()` never raced the printer** — sixty `home()`s
   alternated with sixty twenty-X lines, and every escape on the screen sat
   at the start of a line: both went through the queue, not the fd. ✓

## 5. What it left open

- **The `(0)` rows' stale tail** — `1 5 (0)`, `1 7 (0)`, `1 9 (0)` answer
  S0721's "a later milestone", three words that are now wrong for namespaces
  whose milestones have landed. One sentence, the author's to word, carried
  forward from M13's list.
- **The bare-word fold reached `input` as predicted**: a bare
  `satellite.console.input` folds to `input()` `1 5 2` and READS — M13's
  paths.cpp comment named this milestone as the one to own that, and it now
  does: the fold stands as the rule the author stated for the tiers, applied
  uniformly. If a bare `input` should refuse instead, the fold is where.
- **M22's pause/resume verbs** — the control pipe is the mechanism and a
  byte already means stop; the two more bytes are M22's to build beside
  their only caller. The invariant is in DESIGN §10.1 where M22 will read it.
- **The emergency path still loses queued lines** (M10's documented cost,
  unchanged) — and now also leaves the reader to `_exit`'s cleanup, which is
  what `_exit` is for. M22 stays the emergency path's only registrar.

## 6. What it cost, measured

The reader starts on the first ask and never before, so a program that reads
no input pays nothing — `make startup` after both milestones: **nothing
regressed**, satl's share within ±0.04 ms of the recorded rows. The suite
gained ~6 s: ~3 s of M13's tier floors and ~3 s of M14's pty fixtures typing
at a real terminal, both on purpose.
